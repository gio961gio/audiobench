# Benchmark Audio Datasets & Third-Party Credits

The test samples in `examples/real_datasets/` are curated, short excerpts included strictly for benchmarking, automated regression testing, and reproducible research in accordance with fair use and their respective open licenses.

---

### 1. Guitar Hardware & Virtual Analog Dataset
* **Source:** Alec Wright et al. (*Automated-GuitarAmpModelling* research repository)
  * Paper: *"Real-Time Guitar Amplifier Emulation with Deep Learning"* (Applied Sciences, 2020)
  * Repository: https://github.com/Alec-Wright/Automated-GuitarAmpModelling
* **Hardware Units:**
  * Blackstar HT-1 Tube Guitar Amplifier (clean DI input vs. analog microphone target)
  * Electro-Harmonix Big Muff Pi distortion pedal
* **License:** Open academic research / MIT License.

---

### 2. EBU SQAM (Sound Quality Assessment Material)
* **Source:** European Broadcasting Union (EBU Tech 3253)
  * Official Reference: https://tech.ebu.ch/publications/sqamcd
  * Mirror & Packaging: Automated Measurement Audio Framework (AMAF) by pardeyke (https://github.com/pardeyke/amaf)
* **Content:** Standard acoustic test tracks (parlato multilingua, castanets, glockenspiel, violoncello, harpsichord, opera soprano).
* **Usage:** Freely distributed by the EBU for audio codec evaluation, sound equipment quality assessment, and DSP research.

---

### 3. Zamaudio Acoustic Impulse Responses
* **Source:** Damien Zammit (*zamaudio/impulses*)
  * Repository: https://github.com/zamaudio/impulses
* **Content:** Acoustic impulse responses (48 kHz / 24-bit) recorded in real rooms, vocal studio booths, EMT vintage analog plate reverbs, and stone halls/cathedrals.
* **License:** Creative Commons / Free Audio Assets.
